## EvidenceWeave 0.5.0 — First community release candidate

- Enhances Obsidian's native Graph view, not a separate graph window.
- Hover and click-to-lock PDF previews; drag the title bar and resize all four corners.
- Graph pan and zoom transform PDF panels and relation labels together.
- Edit directional relation labels and safely save to local Markdown, separately from full evidence.
- Preserve PDF reading state during metadata and relation refreshes.
- Optionally retry remote PDFs in memory; browser fallback for publisher restrictions.
- General-purpose settings and local PDF embed recognition. No INSES-specific setup needed.

Desktop Obsidian 1.14.4+. Native Graph renderer internals may change in future app versions. No telemetry or separate sync service.
